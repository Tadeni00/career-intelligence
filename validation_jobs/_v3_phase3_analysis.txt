## Hypothesis testing

### H1 — Threshold mismatch
- Cases scoring >=65 (APPLY band on score alone) that still return SKIP: **2**
    VJ-01 score=80.5 blockers=['Location']
    VJ-B23 score=74.1 blockers=['Advanced degree in quantitative field']
- The thresholds are not the cause. These jobs enter the APPLY band on score and are overridden by the blocker rule, which is a separate gate.

### H2 — Component weighting mismatch
- Dimension ranges above show the discriminating power of each component.
- technical spread: 96.9 points (largest single lever at 40% weight)
- location spread: 80.0 points (at 10% weight)
- Education and location frequently take flat values, so their weight is mostly inert. Weight is not the distortion; input coverage is.

### H3 — Location penalty mismatch
- Cases blocked by location: **8 of 26**
- Location score distribution: {20.0: 8, 55.0: 10, 100.0: 8}
- Cases where location <=55 (penalised or blocked): 18
- Location drives a large share of SKIPs relative to its 10% weight, because it is a *gate* (blocker) and not merely a weighted term.

### H4 — Missing evidence treated too negatively
- Diagnostic state totals across the benchmark: {'F_SUPPORTED': 61, 'A_HARD_BLOCKER': 12, 'E_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN': 96, 'D_TAILORING_GAP': 12, 'B_MATERIAL_GAP': 178, 'C_COMPETITIVE_DISADVANTAGE': 22}
- EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN (96) outnumbers MATERIAL_GAP (178) 0.5x
- The engine already distinguishes 'not established' from 'absent' for PREFERRED/soft skills: they land in `unknowns`, not `gaps`.
- **But REQUIRED skills with no match go to `gaps`, which is presented to the user as a gap, i.e. framed as a deficiency rather than an evidence boundary.**

### H5 — Requirement classification error
- Importance distribution: {'REQUIRED': 351, 'PREFERRED': 94, 'UNKNOWN': 5}
- Requirements extracted per job: min=2 median=16.0 max=32
- EXPERIENCE requirements with no years value: **81**
- These are extracted, then silently discarded by `engine._score_experience`, which only handles requirements that carry `years_required`. They do not score and do not appear as unknowns.

### H6 — Hard blocker semantics
- Blocker source distribution: {'engine._is_hard_location_block -> _score_location': 8, 'engine._score_education': 4}
- Education blockers: 4   Location blockers: 8
- `_score_technical` and `_score_other` can never emit a blocker. A missing REQUIRED *skill* never blocks, but a missing REQUIRED *degree* always does. That asymmetry is undocumented behaviour, not a stated policy.

### H7 — Suitability/action conflation
- Evidence: jobs with technical >=90 and a strong score still return SKIP purely because of a location gate.
- Cases with technical >=80 that are SKIP: **2**
    VJ-01 technical=100 overall=80.5 blockers=['Location']
    VJ-B23 technical=80 overall=74.1 blockers=['Advanced degree in quantitative field']
- This is the clearest supported hypothesis. The engine conflates 'does the evidence support a strong match' with 'should this person apply today'.

### H8 — Evidence coverage too keyword-oriented
- `_skill_names_match` is exact-alias or token-overlap. It cannot connect 'controlled experiments' to 'A/B testing' even where the alias table has an entry, because the entry only fires on surface form.
- Requirements recorded as unmappable to confirmed evidence: 96

### H9 — Experience scoring insufficiently discriminative
- Experience spread: min=80.0 median=84.0 max=100.0
- Cases at experience >=95: **6 of 26**
- `_score_experience` is 50% title similarity + 50% years ratio. Most real postings request 2-5 years against a 6-year candidate, so the ratio saturates at 100 and the dimension stops discriminating between a marginally and a clearly qualified candidate.

### H10 — Other category too broad
- Other spread: min=42.7 median=48.6 max=73.8
- `_score_other` mixes salary, employment type, and every requirement the extractor could not type. Each unverifiable REQUIRED item is assigned a flat 40/100 by construction, so unrelated concepts are averaged as if comparable.
