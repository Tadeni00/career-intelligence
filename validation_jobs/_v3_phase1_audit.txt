## Cross-job blocker provenance
Blockers in this engine originate from exactly two code paths:
  1. engine._score_education — REQUIRED education requirement whose degree
     level exceeds the candidate's highest recorded level.
  2. engine._score_location  — a REQUIRED location requirement containing
     must/required/based in/relocat, when candidate and job locations differ.
_score_technical and _score_other NEVER emit blockers: a missing required
skill is recorded as a gap only.
